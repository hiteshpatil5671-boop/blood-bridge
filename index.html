Blood Donation Awareness

Why is Blood Donation Important?

Blood cannot be manufactured artificially. Every 2 seconds, someone needs a blood transfusion. Donated blood is used for:

  • Emergency trauma and accident victims
  • Cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy
  • Surgical procedures and complications
  • People with severe anemia
  • Blood disorder patients

The Donation Process (45-60 minutes)

  1. Registration - Health history (5-10 min)
  2. Health Screening - Vital signs check (5 min)
  3. Medical Interview - Professional review (5-10 min)
  4. Donation - Blood collection 450-500ml (8-10 min)
  5. Recovery - Rest & refreshments (10-15 min)

Eligibility Guidelines

✓ Can Donate

  • Age: 18-65 years
  • Weight: ≥50 kg
  • Good health
  • Normal BP

✗ Cannot Donate

  • Pregnant women
  • Recent surgery
  • Active infection
  • High-risk conditions

About BloodBridge

Our Mission

BloodBridge is a digital community platform connecting blood donors with recipients, raising awareness about blood donation, and facilitating life-saving emergency connections.

Our Impact

  • Donor Registration for community participation
  • Blood Request System for emergency requirements
  • Blood Camp Information for awareness activities
  • Blood Group Search using registered donor data
